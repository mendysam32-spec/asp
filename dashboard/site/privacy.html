TheRepublic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Your privacy matters to us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information TheRepublic collects, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data — limited profile information you provide when connecting a social account (e.g., username and numeric account IDs).
  • Content drafts — drafts and ideas you create with our tools are stored locally to your device and/or your workspace.
  • Usage data — basic analytics such as which features are used, to help improve the experience.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve the service.
  • To generate and schedule content at your request.
  • To communicate with you about the service.

3. Third-Party Services

When you connect third-party platforms (such as TikTok, YouTube, or other social networks), those platforms' own privacy policies also apply to your data while it is processed by them. We only access the minimum data needed to provide the feature you request.

4. AI Processing

Some features use AI services. Text you submit may be processed by third-party AI providers to generate content suggestions. We do not sell your data.

5. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Credentials for third-party accounts are stored securely and used only to operate the service. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

7. Children's Privacy

The service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with a new revision date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the contact information provided on this site.
